Delivery

Five stages, each with something you keep

Innovate 01–02 Automate 03–04 Scale 05
  1. 01

    Discover

    We sit with the people who do the task and measure where the time and cost go.

    You get: a map of the task, a ranked shortlist, and our written advice
  2. 02

    Prototype

    A working first version within days, tested on real examples from your past work.

    You get: the prototype, the examples we tested, and where it struggled
  3. 03

    Build and connect

    The full version, connected to your apps, in accounts in your name from day one.

    You get: the finished automation in your accounts, with documentation
  4. 04

    Launch

    A small trial first, measured against the success targets we agreed in writing.

    You get: trial results, and our advice to expand, adjust or stop
  5. 05

    Run and grow

    We watch how it performs, fix problems, and add more — or hand it fully to your team.

    You get: monitoring, performance reports, agreed response time

Principles

Four promises on every project

You own everything

Accounts, keys and data are in your name from day one. We write the documentation as we go. If we part ways tomorrow, nothing has to move.

Clear rules for your data

We decide early who can see what, where data is stored, how long it is kept, and when the automation must stop and ask a person.

Measured results

We agree how to measure success before we build, and report against it afterwards — even when the result falls short.

What we won't do

Automate a task that won't pay for itself. Build something we can't hand over. Or claim a system is smarter than it is.

Practical questions

What working with us looks like

How long does a first project take?Two to three weeks to study the task and build a prototype. Four to eight weeks for the first full version, depending on how many apps it connects.
What do you need from us?Access to the task and the people who do it, some sample data, and one person who can make decisions. The last one matters most.
Which tools will you use?Usually Claude for the AI parts and n8n or Zapier to connect your apps. We choose based on your apps, your volume and who will look after it. See how we build
Where does our data go?Into your own accounts, in US regions where the provider offers them. Every outside service is named and agreed with you first. Sensitive records are tested on copies with personal details removed.
What will it cost to run?Tool costs depend on how much work flows through. Before we build, we give you a monthly estimate at today's volume and at double it.
What if it doesn't work?That's what the trial is for: to find out cheaply. If the results don't justify going further, we recommend stopping, and you keep everything built so far.
Can our own team look after it?Yes, that's the goal. Support is available, but nothing we build depends on us to keep running.
