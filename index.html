How we build it

The tools behind the work

We pick the simplest tool that does the job well. You don't need to learn any of them, but you'll always know what is running.

AI that reads and writes

Claude

An AI model made by Anthropic. It reads emails, documents and PDFs, pulls out the details, sorts requests and drafts replies.

Runs the steps

n8n

A workflow tool that runs each step in order, retries when something fails, and can run on your own server. Best for bigger or busier workflows.

Quick connections

Zapier

Connects thousands of popular apps with little or no code. Best for simple workflows your own team wants to change later.

When they fit better

Other tools

Make for visual workflows. Power Automate for Microsoft 365. Apps Script for Google Sheets and Gmail. Python for anything custom.

One method, many problems

If your team does the same task by hand every week, it can probably run by itself. A few examples:

Accounting and tax

When a client emails a receipt or invoice…

Then Claude reads the vendor, date and amount, adds them to your bookkeeping sheet, and flags anything unusual for your staff.

Gmail · Claude · n8n · Google Sheets

Online shop

When an order comes in…

Then stock is updated everywhere you sell. When an item runs low, a draft order is ready for you to approve and send to the supplier.

Shopify · Zapier · Google Sheets · Email

Restaurant or café

When the day closes…

Then sales and waste are pulled from your till system and sent to your phone as a one-page summary.

Till export · n8n · Python · Email

Professional services

When a new client fills in your intake form…

Then a client folder is created, a first draft of the engagement letter is written from your template, and a review task goes to the right person.

Web form · Zapier · Claude · Google Drive

Customer support

When a customer asks a question by email or chat…

Then Claude drafts an answer from your FAQs and policies. Your staff check it and click send.

Help desk · Claude · n8n

Any team

When it's Monday at 8am…

Then numbers from your accounting, sales and project apps are combined into one short report in your inbox.

QuickBooks · Python · n8n · Email
