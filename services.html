How we build it

Step by step: one automation

Every automation we build follows the same seven steps. Here is what happens at each one, and the tools we use.

  1. 01

    Start

    Something starts the workflow: a new email, a form, a file in a folder, a new row in a sheet, or a set time. Many apps send an instant message (a “webhook”) the moment something happens, so nothing waits.

    Tools: n8n or Zapier triggers, webhooks, schedules
  2. 02

    Collect

    The workflow fetches any extra details it needs from your other apps through their APIs — the official, secure way apps share data with each other.

    Tools: app APIs, Google Sheets, Airtable, your CRM or accounting software
  3. 03

    Understand

    Claude reads the email, document or PDF. It follows written instructions and answers in a fixed format, like filling in a form, so the next step always finds each detail in the same place.

    Tools: Claude, through Anthropic's API
  4. 04

    Check

    Simple rules check the result. Is every field filled in? Is the amount over your limit? Does this customer exist? Anything that fails a rule is stopped.

    Tools: n8n or Zapier filters, Python for complex rules
  5. 05

    Approve

    When a decision matters, a person gets a short message with Approve and Reject buttons. Nothing important happens without that click.

    Tools: Slack, Microsoft Teams or email
  6. 06

    Act

    The workflow does the work: updates records, sends emails, creates documents and files them in the right folder.

    Tools: your own apps, through their APIs
  7. 07

    Log and alert

    Every run is recorded: what came in, what was decided, what was done. If a step fails, it tries again. If it still fails, a person is told what broke.

    Tools: n8n run history, a log sheet, email or Slack alerts

Which tool, when

We aren't tied to any one tool. We choose based on your apps, how much work flows through, and who will look after it.

Simple tasks your team wants to change laterZapier. Quick to set up, connects thousands of apps, easy to edit. Costs more as volume grows.
Many steps, high volume, or data must stay on your own servern8n. Handles branches, loops and retries well, and can run on your own server.
Reading, sorting, summarising or drafting textClaude. Understands emails, documents and PDFs, and follows written instructions. We use other AI models when a task suits them better.
Your business runs on Microsoft 365Power Automate. Works closely with Outlook, Excel and Teams.
Your business runs on Google WorkspaceApps Script. Small scripts inside Sheets, Gmail and Drive.
Visual workflows with many branchesMake. A drag-and-drop builder that is easy to follow.
Anything the tools above can't do wellPython. Custom code for tricky data cleaning, calculations or reports.

Your accounts, your keys

Your Claude API key, your Zapier or n8n account and your app logins are all in your business's name. We work in them with access you can remove at any time.

Your data, named and approved

Before any of your data goes to a service, we tell you which one, what it receives and where it is hosted. You approve it in writing first.
